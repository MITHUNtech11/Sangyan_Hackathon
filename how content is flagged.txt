  ### 1. 🔴 How Content is Labeled as DANGER / HIGH RISK (FALSE / POTENTIALLY_MISLEADING)

  Content is immediately classified as DANGER if it triggers any high-severity regulatory violation or confirmed fraud pattern:

  1. Guaranteed Returns on Market Assets:
      • SEBI Regulation: Regulated entities (advisers, brokers, analysts) are legally prohibited by SEBI from assuring or guaranteeing fixed returns on equities, mutual funds,   
      or derivatives.
      • Trigger words: "Guaranteed 40% monthly", "Assured returns", "Zero risk profit".
  2. Deceptive Regulatory Claims (Fake SEBI Endorsement):
      • Checked via inspect_sebi_registration(). SEBI registers entities, but never approves specific schemes, trading tips, or Telegram channels. Claims like "100% SEBI
      approved Telegram" are instantly flagged as false.
  3. Institutional Sub-Account Scams:
      • Refuted directly by SEBI Advisory PR No. 04/2024. Indian retail investors are legally barred from operating under FPI institutional sub-accounts or WhatsApp VIP quotas.  
  4. Suspicious Payment & Upfront Demands:
      • Requests to transfer money to personal UPI IDs (@okhdfc), payment for "VIP group activation", or crypto-doubling bots (RBI Fraud Warning).
  5. High-Risk Phishing URLs:
      • The url_inspector.py evaluates domains against deceptive typosquatting, shorteners, and IP-based links.

  ──────
  ### 2. 🟡 How Content is Labeled as MISLEADING / CAUTION (MISLEADING / NEEDS_VERIFICATION)

  Content is classified as MISLEADING when claims are partially true but deliberately omit risks or exploit psychological manipulation:

  1. Selective / Cherry-Picked Past Performance:
      • Presenting a past bull-run return (e.g., "Fund gave 25% last year") as proof that it is "100% safe" or "the safest asset" without mandatory SEBI risk disclaimers.        
  2. Artificial Scarcity & Urgency Tactics:
      • Detected using the Psychological Manipulation Pillar in taxonomy.py:
          • "Only 5 slots left in our VIP group"
          • "Act within 2 hours before closing"

  3. Promotion vs. Education Intent Score:
      • Evaluated by classify_intent_and_evidence(). If content has a Promotion Score > 50% and relies on unverified assertions, it is flagged for caution.
  4. Unverified Claims:
      • Claims made by third parties with no verifiable SEBI registration number (INH/INA) or audited financial filing.

  ──────
  ### 3. 🟢 How Content is Labeled as SAFE / AUTHENTIC (TRUE / NO_OBVIOUS_SIGNALS)

  Content is labeled as SAFE only when it meets strict regulatory standards:

  1. Mandatory Statutory Risk Disclosures:
      • Explicitly includes standard regulatory caveats: "Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme-related documents carefully" or "Past
      performance does not guarantee future results".
  2. Zero Manipulative Red Flags:
      • No guaranteed return promises, no pressure tactics, no private UPI requests, and no deceptive URLs.
  3. RAG Ground Truth Match:
      • The HybridRAGRetriever verifies that the text matches genuine advisory and educational bulletins published by SEBI, RBI, NSE, or BSE.

  ### The Dual Intelligence Engine Behind the Labels

  To ensure 100% reliability during demos and hackathon evaluations, SANGYAN uses a two-tier evaluation system in analyzer.py:125-290:

  1. Live Gemini 2.5 Flash Pipeline:
      • Stage 1: Extracts individual atomic claims from the text.
      • Stage 2: Detects red flags against the 5-Pillar Regulatory Taxonomy.
      • Stage 3: Synthesizes multilingual explanations and action items.
  2. Deterministic Offline RAG & Rule Engine:
      • If offline or during latency spikes, the system executes BM25 keyword + semantic concept scoring against indexed regulatory circulars and SEBI regex patterns. It never   
      hallucinates a verdict.
